One night, a little cloud-creature couldn't settle down. Pip listened carefully and discovered that sometimes the best magic begins with noticing what a friend needs.

Pip tried tapping tiny notes on a music box. Plink, plonk, twinkle. Soon, a soft little melody filled the room, and the cloud-creature's worries drifted away like feathers.
